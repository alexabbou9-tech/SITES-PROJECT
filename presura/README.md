## Project overview

Presura is a live ecommerce project designed around a Ghana-first shopping experience. The storefront combines a large multi-category catalog with mobile-first merchandising, local payment messaging, delivery/returns information, product bundles, product reviews, and product-detail pages.

### What the project demonstrates

- Shopify storefront design and merchandising
- Mobile-first ecommerce UX
- Large product catalog organization
- Product and collection page structure
- Ghana-market positioning
- MTN Mobile Money / Paystack checkout messaging
- Product bundles and upsell merchandising
- Product review presentation
- Shipping and returns communication
- Custom product imagery and visual direction
- Responsive storefront design

## Selected storefront sections

The live site includes:

- Hair & Styling
- Skin & Light Therapy
- Hair Removal
- Body & Massage
- Lips & Beauty
- Home, Pet & Bath
- Fans & Cooling
- Lighting & Ambience

The homepage also includes curated starting categories, product recommendations, kit building, trust/returns information, customer reviews, and transparent shipping information.
