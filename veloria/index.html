A four-step glow ritual.

Designed to work in sequence or alone. Each layer stays sheer, flexible and easy to adjust throughout the day.

01

Prep

Press a thin layer of Afterglow onto clean skin for a smooth hydrated base.

02

Veil

Blend Cloud Veil from the center of the face outward using fingertips.

03

Reflect

Tap Liquid Light onto cheekbones, lids and collarbones where light naturally lands.

04

Repeat

Add another sheer layer only where you want more polish. Skin should still look like skin.
